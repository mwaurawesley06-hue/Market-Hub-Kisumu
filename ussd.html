How the session works

  1. Send empty

    Opens the main menu: 1 price check, 2 SMS subscription.

  2. Option 2

    Asks for a phone number, then a commodity, then daily or weekly, then confirms.

  3. Confirm

    Inserts the subscription and texts a 6-digit verification code. Confirm it on the SMS alerts page to switch alerts on.
